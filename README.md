   # Air Power: Predictive Maintenance & Fleet Availability (SIH 2026, PS 26249)

   Prototype that predicts engine Remaining Useful Life (RUL) and ranks which aircraft
   to maintain first, based on failure risk, spares and maintenance slots.

   ## Data (clearly labelled)
   - Engine data: NASA C-MAPSS FD001, a PUBLIC SIMULATED turbofan dataset. It is NOT real Air Force data.
   - Spares stock and maintenance slots: SIMULATED by us for demonstration.
